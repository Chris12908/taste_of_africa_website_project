West Africa

West African cooking is built around starchy staples and deep, layered sauces. Meals are shared from a central pot, and heat from scotch bonnet peppers is part of the balance, not an afterthought.

Staples

  • Cassava, yam and plantain, boiled, pounded or fried
  • Rice and millet
  • Palm oil, groundnuts and black-eyed peas
  • Scotch bonnet, grains of selim and ginger

Cooking techniques

  • Pounding starches in a mortar to make fufu
  • Deep frying ripe plantain in hot oil
  • Slow stewing in tomato and pepper base
  • Open-fire grilling of fish and meat
