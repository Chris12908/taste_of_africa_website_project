Stewed Beans

East Africa, Rwanda

About the dish

Beans (ibishyimbo in Kinyarwanda) are on the table at almost every Rwandan meal, usually next to plantain, rice or cassava. They are simmered until the cooking liquid thickens into a rich gravy, then finished with a quick fry of onion and tomato. Cheap, filling and high in protein, they are everyday food.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 2 cups dried red beans, soaked overnight
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 5 cups water
  • salt and fresh coriander to finish

Cooking instructions

Cook the beans
  1. Drain the soaked beans, cover with the water and simmer for 60 to 90 minutes until tender.
Make the flavour base
  1. Fry the onion in the oil until soft.
  2. Add garlic, carrot and tomatoes and cook 8 minutes into a thick sauce.
Combine
  1. Tip the sauce into the beans with salt.
  2. Simmer for 20 minutes until the liquid thickens. Finish with coriander.
