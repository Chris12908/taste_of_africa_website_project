East Africa

East African food mixes highland farming, Indian Ocean trade and pastoral traditions. Ethiopia has its own spice system, Kenya and Rwanda lean on beans, greens and maize, and almost everything is eaten by hand.

Staples

  • Teff (injera), maize (ugali) and sorghum
  • Beans, lentils and chickpeas
  • Berbere, cardamom and turmeric
  • Bananas, cooking plantain and cassava

Cooking techniques

  • Fermenting teff batter for injera
  • Dry-cooking onions before adding spice
  • Slow simmering in a covered pot
  • Roasting over charcoal
