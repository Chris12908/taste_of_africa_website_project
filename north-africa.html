North Africa

North African cooking sits between the Mediterranean, the Sahara and the Arab world. Grains, olives and chickpeas are everyday foods, and spice blends are layered gently for warmth rather than heat.

Staples

  • Semolina couscous and wheat bread
  • Chickpeas, olives and preserved lemons
  • Ras el hanout, cumin and saffron
  • Dates, almonds and olive oil

Cooking techniques

  • Steaming couscous over a simmering stew
  • Slow cooking in a clay tagine
  • Preserving lemons and olives in salt
  • Baking bread in communal ovens
