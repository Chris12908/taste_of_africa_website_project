Fufu

West Africa, Ghana

About the dish

Fufu is the centrepiece of a Ghanaian family meal. Cassava and unripe plantain are boiled, then pounded in a wooden mortar until elastic. You do not chew it: you pinch off a piece, dip it in light soup or groundnut soup, and swallow. The rhythm of pounding is a social event in many homes.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 500 g cassava, peeled and chopped
  • 2 unripe plantains, peeled
  • 1 cup water, plus more for pounding
  • pinch of salt

Cooking instructions

Prepare
  1. Cut the cassava and plantains into chunks.
  2. Boil in lightly salted water for about 25 minutes, until a fork goes in with no resistance.
Pound
  1. Drain, keeping a little of the cooking water.
  2. Pound in a mortar, or blend in a food processor, adding splashes of water until the dough is smooth and stretchy.
Serve
  1. Wet your hands and shape the dough into balls.
  2. Serve warm with light soup, groundnut soup or palm nut soup.
