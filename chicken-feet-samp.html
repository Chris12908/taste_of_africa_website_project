Chicken Feet and Samp

Southern Africa, South Africa

About the dish

Chicken feet, nicknamed 'walkie talkies' in South Africa, are a loved street-food and home-cooking ingredient. They are simmered until soft and sticky, then served with samp: dried, crushed maize kernels cooked slowly until tender. It is affordable, filling food from the township tradition of wasting nothing.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 1 kg chicken feet, cleaned and nails trimmed
  • 2 cups samp, soaked overnight
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp mild curry powder
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 6 cups water
  • salt to taste

Cooking instructions

Cook the samp
  1. Drain the soaked samp and cover with the water.
  2. Simmer for 2 to 3 hours, topping up with water, until soft.
Cook the chicken feet
  1. Boil the feet in salted water for 20 minutes, then drain.
  2. Fry the onion and garlic in the oil, add curry powder, then the tomatoes.
  3. Add the feet and a cup of water and simmer for 45 minutes until tender.
Serve
  1. Spoon the samp onto plates and ladle the chicken feet and sauce over the top.
