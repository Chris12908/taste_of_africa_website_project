Chicken Kienyeji

East Africa, Kenya

About the dish

Kienyeji means 'of the homestead' in Kiswahili, and it refers to free-range chicken raised in the village. The meat is firmer and more flavourful than farm chicken, so it is simmered slowly in a base of onion, tomato and turmeric. In Kenya it is usually served with ugali and sautéed greens.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 1 kg free-range chicken pieces
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 3 ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 3 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 0.5 cup fresh coriander, chopped
  • salt to taste

Cooking instructions

Brown
  1. Heat the oil and brown the chicken pieces on all sides. Set aside.
Make the base
  1. In the same pot, cook the onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger and turmeric, then the tomatoes. Cook until they break down into a thick paste.
Simmer and serve
  1. Return the chicken, add the water and salt, cover, and simmer for 45 to 60 minutes.
  2. Stir in the coriander and serve with ugali or rice.
