About us

Our mission is to keep African food heritage alive and easy to cook.

Tastes of Africa collects family recipes, regional food knowledge and the stories behind them, so that dishes like fufu and doro wat are as familiar to a home cook in Kigali as in Kansas.

A short history of African cuisine

  1. Before 1000 CEFarmers across the continent domesticate sorghum, millet, teff and yams.
  2. 1000 to 1500Indian Ocean and trans-Saharan trade bring cardamom, rice and new cooking methods to coastal and desert cities.
  3. 1500 to 1800Crops from the Americas, including cassava, maize and chili peppers, reshape what the continent eats.
  4. TodayThe African diaspora carries jollof, injera and fufu to restaurants in London, Washington and Sao Paulo.

A food landmark: Makola Market, Accra

One of West Africa's busiest markets, and the place to find the cassava, plantain and peppers used in our recipes.
