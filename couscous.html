Moroccan Couscous

North Africa, Morocco

About the dish

Friday is couscous day in Morocco, when families gather around one large dish. Tiny grains of semolina are steamed until light, then topped with carrots, squash, courgette and chickpeas simmered in a broth scented with ras el hanout and turmeric. The vegetable version is a classic in its own right, not a substitute for the meat one.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 2 cups couscous
  • 1.5 cups cooked chickpeas
  • 2 carrots, cut in chunks
  • 2 courgettes, cut in chunks
  • 300 g pumpkin or butternut squash, cubed
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout
  • 0.5 tsp turmeric
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cups vegetable stock
  • salt to taste

Cooking instructions

Make the vegetable stew
  1. Soften the onion in the olive oil. Add ras el hanout, turmeric and the cinnamon stick for 1 minute.
  2. Add tomatoes, carrots, pumpkin and the stock. Simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Add the courgettes and chickpeas and cook 10 more minutes.
Prepare the couscous
  1. Put the couscous in a bowl and add 2 cups of hot stock from the pot. Cover for 5 minutes.
  2. Fluff with a fork, rubbing in a little oil so the grains stay separate.
Serve
  1. Mound the couscous on a wide plate.
  2. Arrange the vegetables and chickpeas on top and ladle over the broth.
