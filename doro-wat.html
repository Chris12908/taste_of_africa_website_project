Doro Wat

East Africa, Ethiopia

About the dish

Doro wat is served at weddings, holidays and Sunday tables across Ethiopia. The sauce starts with a mountain of onions cooked down without oil, then berbere spice blend and niter kibbeh (spiced clarified butter). Whole boiled eggs go in at the end. It is eaten with injera, torn by hand and used as the spoon.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 1 kg chicken pieces, skinned
  • 4 large red onions, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp berbere spice blend
  • 4 tbsp niter kibbeh or butter
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, peeled
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • salt to taste

Cooking instructions

Marinate
  1. Rub the chicken with lemon juice and a little salt. Rest for 20 minutes.
Build the sauce
  1. Cook the onions in a dry heavy pot over medium heat for 20 minutes, stirring, until soft and reduced.
  2. Add the niter kibbeh, garlic and ginger. Stir for 3 minutes.
  3. Stir in the berbere and cook 10 minutes on low heat, adding splashes of water so it does not stick.
Simmer and serve
  1. Add the chicken and the water. Cover and simmer for 40 minutes.
  2. Prick the boiled eggs with a fork and add them for the last 10 minutes.
  3. Serve with injera.
