Southern Africa

Southern African food is built on maize, meat cooked over fire, and spice traditions brought by Indian, Malay and European communities. Sharing a meal around the fire, the braai, is central to social life.

Staples

  • Maize as pap and samp
  • Beans and sorghum
  • Cape curry powders and chili relish
  • Beef, lamb, chicken and offal

Cooking techniques

  • Braai: grilling over open coals
  • Slow boiling samp for hours
  • Cooking in a three-legged potjie pot
  • Sun-drying meat as biltong
