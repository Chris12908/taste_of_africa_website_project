Fried Plantain

West Africa, Nigeria

About the dish

Known as dodo in Nigeria and kelewele in Ghana when seasoned, fried plantain is street food, side dish and snack in one. Fully ripe plantain has the most sugar, so it browns fast in hot palm oil or vegetable oil. A sprinkle of ginger and chili turns it into kelewele.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 3 ripe plantains (yellow with black spots)
  • 1 cup oil for frying
  • 1 tsp grated ginger (optional)
  • 0.5 tsp chili flakes (optional)
  • pinch of salt

Cooking instructions

Prepare
  1. Peel the plantains and slice them on a slant into pieces about 1 cm thick.
  2. Toss with salt, and the ginger and chili if you like.
Fry
  1. Heat the oil over medium heat.
  2. Fry the slices in batches for 2 to 3 minutes per side until deep golden.
Serve
  1. Drain on paper towels.
  2. Eat hot, alone or beside jollof rice, stew or stewed beans.
